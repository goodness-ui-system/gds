01 · The Mark

Master geometry

The Node monogram — a “G” traced as a connected network. One fixed skeleton, one ink color. All approved usage derives from this single construction.

04 · Protection

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of one node on all sides. Nothing — type, edges, other marks — enters the dashed zone.

The mark stays legible down to 16px as a favicon. Below that, node density collapses — use the solid-ground avatar rather than shrinking further.
